AllCalc

One app for every everyday calculation: a full calculator, unit converters and more than 120 tools for money, health, maths, dates and splitting bills. Free on Android.

Get it on Google Play ↗Privacy policy ↗

What's inside

  • Calculator — basic and scientific, with history and a home-screen widget
  • Converters — length, weight, currency, temperature, area, data and more
  • Finance — EMI, loans, SIP, FD, RD, PPF, GST, income tax, CAGR, ROI, savings and retirement planning
  • Split & Share — split bills by shares or items, and settle group trip expenses
  • Health & fitness — BMI, BMR, TDEE, body fat, macros and more, in kg or lb, cm or ft
  • Maths — geometry, trigonometry, algebra, matrices, equations and statistics
  • Everyday — age, dates, time, attendance, marks, unit prices and recipe scaling

Made to be easy

  • Results update as you type — no "calculate" button
  • Favourites, recent tools and remembered inputs
  • Share or copy any result in one tap
  • Dark mode, and metric, US or UK units
  • No account, and your calculations stay on your device
