BoloBee: English through Hindi

Learn to speak everyday English through Hindi, one short lesson at a time, with listening practice, smart review and a cheerful bee. No ads, and it works offline.

What's inside

  • Short lessons — five exercise types: choose the meaning, listen and pick, fill the blank, build the sentence, match the pairs
  • Real-life English — greetings, family, food, numbers, daily routine, places in town, shopping and money
  • Remember it — mistakes come back for review, with spaced review after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days
  • Saved words and listening practice from lessons you've finished
  • Stay motivated — daily goals, XP and streaks

Made to be fair

  • No account, no ads and no in-app purchases
  • Works offline; new courses can be downloaded
  • Simple Hindi explanations of tricky points
