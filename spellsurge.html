SPELLSURGE: Magic Survival

Pick a hero, survive waves of magical foes, fuse spells into new powers and take down the boss. An isometric survival game where every run builds differently.

Coming soon to Google PlayPrivacy policy ↗

What's inside

  • Three heroes — Ignis (fire), Glacia (ice) and Volt (lightning), each with a mastery tree
  • Three stages — Emberfall, Frostveil Glacier and Gloomwood, each ending in a boss fight
  • Arcana and fusions — combine elements into Steamburst, Thunderflare and Static Frost
  • Daily quests, achievements and a codex of everything you've met
  • Soul Shards — earn them every run to unlock heroes and mastery

Made to be fair

  • Plays fully offline, with no account
  • No ads during play: the only ad is an optional one to double a run's Soul Shards
  • Premium sells no power: just no ads and shards doubled automatically
