DarkChess

Chess with a darker edge: play a strong on-device engine at the level you choose, work through a campaign and daily-style puzzles, and track every game. Free on Android.

Coming soon to Google PlayPrivacy policy ↗

What's inside

  • Play the computer — the Stockfish engine runs on your phone, from beginner to very strong
  • Campaign — climb through opponents, earn crowns and coins
  • Puzzles — tactics to solve, with your attempts and stats tracked
  • Game history and statistics — replay your games and see how you're improving
  • Board themes — including premium themes such as Obsidian Gold, Neon Cyber, Ice Kingdom and Celestial

Made to be fair

  • Plays fully offline, with no account
  • No ads on the board while you play
  • Premium removes ads
