FitForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

About this policy

This policy explains how the FitForge Android app (package com.ssamiyal.fitforge), published by SAMIYAL Technologies ("SAMIYAL", "we", "us"), collects and uses information.

In short: you can use FitForge as a guest, with everything kept on your device. If you sign in with your email, your profile, workout history and custom plans are also stored in our cloud database so you can restore them on another phone. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells a Premium subscription through Google Play.

Guest use

As a guest, your profile, workouts, plans, settings and reminders are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling deletes them.

Your account and cloud backup

To sign in, you enter your email address and we send you a one-time code. If you sign in, we store the following in our cloud database, run on Supabase, so it can be backed up and restored:

  • Your account email address
  • Your profile: name, and if you choose to enter them, gender, age, height, weight, fitness goal, experience level, workout days per week, where you train, units, profile photo, address and mobile number
  • Your completed workouts: date, duration, plan name, exercises, sets, reps and weights
  • Custom workout plans you create

Each account's data is protected so that only that account can read or change it. A workout in progress, your settings and your reminders stay on your device and are not uploaded. Supabase processes this data on our behalf under the Supabase Privacy Policy.

We use this information only to provide your account, back up and restore your data, and respond to you. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Exercise library

FitForge downloads its exercise library from our server. The request contains no personal information, but like any internet request it reveals your device's IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

FitForge is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob: banner ads in the app. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (for example, device model, operating system version, app version)
  • Information about ads shown and your interactions with them

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings.

Premium subscription (Google Play)

FitForge Premium is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app whether your Google account has an active subscription. You can manage or cancel it from the app or in Google Play. Google handles payment information under Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): workout reminders and the rest-timer alert.
  • Run at startup: to re-schedule your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: the rest-timer buzz.
  • Internet: sign-in, cloud backup, the exercise library and ads.

Retention and security

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit what we store to what the app needs.

Deleting your account and data

Signed-in users can delete their account in the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, profile photo, workout history and custom plans from our cloud database, and clears them from the device.

If you can't use the app, see how to request account deletion, or email ssamiyaltechnologies@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. Guests can delete their data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Children's privacy

FitForge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes; the date at the top shows the latest version. If you have questions about privacy in FitForge, email ssamiyaltechnologies@gmail.com.
