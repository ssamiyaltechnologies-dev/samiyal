Delete your FitForge account

How to delete your FitForge account and the data stored with it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open FitForge and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account, type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't use the app?

Email ssamiyaltechnologies@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my FitForge account. We'll confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in email
  • Your profile, including your profile photo, address and mobile number
  • Your workout history and custom workout plans

Nothing is kept after deletion. A Google Play subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account; cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Guests

If you never signed in, nothing is stored on our servers. Clear FitForge's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to delete your data.
