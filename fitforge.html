FitForge

Your workout log and training plans in one place: follow built-in programmes or build your own, log every set, rest with a timer and watch your progress grow.

What's inside

  • Workout plans — built-in programmes for your goal and level, or create your own
  • Log every set — reps and weight, with a rest timer and progression suggestions
  • Exercise library — find exercises by muscle group
  • Progress — history, charts and personal records
  • Reminders — workout-day notifications at the time you choose

Made to be fair

  • Try it as a guest, or sign in with your email to back up your data
  • Export your workout history at any time
  • Delete your account and cloud data from inside the app
