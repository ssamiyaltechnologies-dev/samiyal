RIDGEBOUND: Wild Run

A physics driving game where skill beats luck. Climb steep mountains, fly off kickers, chain flips into a combo and land it clean, across 36 endless stages and nine worlds. Free on Android.

Coming soon to Google PlayPrivacy policy ↗

What's inside

  • 36 endless stages in nine worlds, from Green Highlands and Rusty Quarry to Frostpeak, Sunscorch Desert, Sky Launch and the low-gravity Crystal Moon
  • 33 vehicles — buggies, dirt bikes, trucks, tractors, a snow crawler and rocket-powered machines, each with its own handling
  • Real upgrades — engine, tyres, suspension and air control; some mountains need an upgraded vehicle to get over
  • Trail Flow — chain big air, flips and wheelies into a combo; every clean flip raises your bonus
  • Watch your gas — grab cans to keep going, and push for all three distance stars
  • Daily reward, missions, achievements and ghost racing against your own best run

Made to be fair

  • Plays fully offline, with no account
  • Everything can be earned by playing; purchases are optional
  • The Starter Bundle removes banner and between-run ads
  • Left- or right-handed controls, graphics quality for older phones
  • English, Hindi and Spanish

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. If you reinstall the game or move to a new phone, open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account to get your Starter Bundle back. For a refund, see Google Play's refund help, or email us.

Support

Found a bug, stuck on a mountain, or have an idea for a vehicle? Email ssamiyaltechnologies@gmail.com with your phone model and what happened, or visit our support page.
